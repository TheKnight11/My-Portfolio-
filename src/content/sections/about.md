I approach engineering as a discipline of care as much as calculation — every beam, contour line, and quantity estimate ultimately serves people. My work sits at the crossing point of three commitments: rigorous technical grounding in civil engineering, active leadership in community and youth development, and a habit of analytical thinking sharpened by puzzles, patterns, and logic.

Whether I'm verifying reinforcement on a residential site, facilitating a youth leadership session, or solving a Pyraminx against the clock, the same underlying method applies: observe carefully, break the problem into its true components, and act with integrity toward the people the outcome affects.
