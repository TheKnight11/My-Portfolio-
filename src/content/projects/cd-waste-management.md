This study examines current construction and demolition waste practices in Nepal and where practical intervention could reduce landfill burden and material loss. The methodology combines structured field surveys with empirical analysis, drawing on more than 200 respondents across active construction sites. Target publication: the Himalayan Journal of Applied Science and Engineering (HiJASE).
